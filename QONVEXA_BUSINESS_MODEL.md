# Qonvexa business model

PROJECT ASSUMPTIONS, not verified market research: the entry Growth Audit defaults to $149, adjustable with `AUDIT_PRICE_CENTS`; service estimates use the user's requested ranges. FIX approximately $299–699, GROW $750–1,500, AUTOMATE $1,500–3,500+. Individual catalogue services may span these tiers; quote scope determines the fee.

Free preview: up to three HIGH-confidence findings with evidence and freshness. No finding is fabricated to meet a quota. A paid audit exposes the structured report, coverage limitations and recommendations. Public HTML alone cannot verify CRM delivery, form completion or analytics receipt.

Implementation: preflight → scope/quote → acceptance → verified payment → final access/preflight → allowlisted execution → outcome QA → handoff. In THIS release the flow stops transparently at planning: all live executors and implementation checkout are unavailable. This is not an autonomous revenue-generating production service yet.

Customer-owned services: CRM, CMS, hosting/domain, AI/API, messaging, telephony, analytics. Qonvexa charges only for audit/setup/implementation/QA/handoff. Third-party estimates are `null` until verified, not fabricated dollar figures; billing owner is CUSTOMER. No subscription markup.

Initial niche configurations: Dental, Med Spa, HVAC, Roofing. Initial outreach country rules: US; UK only reviewed Ltd/LLP B2B candidates. CA/AU disabled. Country flags are product policy, not legal advice or a consent guarantee. Start with 100 manually selected businesses, real findings and reviewed drafts; no automatic sending. Evaluate replies, views, paid interest, opt-outs/complaints before expansion. No invented conversion benchmark.

Cost model: internal variable cost includes worker compute estimates and future provider usage; customer-owned SaaS is not internal cost. Default configurable caps: free scan $0.10, paid audit $5, small implementation $20, large $50. Only scan reservation is integrated with the current worker; implementation caps require future live execution wiring. `QONVEXA_WORKER_USD_PER_SECOND` is a project assumption, not a measured provider invoice. Actual cost remains null until settled; no zero-cost claims. Gross-margin/revenue reconciliation dashboard is future work.

Excluded automatic products: arbitrary redesign/SaaS, brand identity, server migration, legacy ERP, penetration-test certification, legal/accounting certification, guaranteed rankings/AI recommendations/revenue. No fake reviews, local presence, testimonial counts or scarcity. Optional future support must be separately scoped; quote warranty defaults to 14 days but does not create an unlimited support commitment.
